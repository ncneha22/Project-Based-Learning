# Introduction and Need of Inter-Process Communication (IPC)

Inter-Process Communication (IPC) is a mechanism that allows two or more processes to communicate and exchange data with each other.

A process is a program that is currently running. Since processes usually have separate memory spaces, IPC provides methods for them to safely share information and coordinate their activities.

#Need for Inter-Process Communication
IPC is needed because:
•Data Sharing – Allows processes to exchange and share data.
•Process Coordination – Helps processes work together in a proper order.
•Resource Sharing – Allows processes to share resources such as files, memory, and devices.
•Faster Execution – Different processes can perform parts of a task simultaneously.
•Synchronization – Prevents conflicts when multiple processes access shared resources.
•Communication – Helps processes send and receive messages or information.

#Common IPC Methods
  •Pipes
  •Message Queues
  •Shared Memory
  •Semaphores
  •Sockets
